# Curriculum (each day builds on the previous ones)

1. Classes & the object model: attributes, instance vs class state, `__repr__`/`__eq__`/`__hash__`
2. Properties, descriptors & encapsulation (done: Day 002)
3. Inheritance, `super()`, MRO, composition vs inheritance (done: Day 003)
4. Dunder methods for containers & operators (`__len__`, `__getitem__`, `__add__`, ...) (done: Day 004)
5. Iterators & generators (building on custom containers) (done: Day 005)
6. Dataclasses & type hints (done: Day 006)
7. Context managers & decorators (done: Day 007)
8. Exceptions & custom exception hierarchies (done: Day 008)
9. Protocols/ABCs & duck typing (done: Day 009)
10. `collections`, `itertools`, `functools` (done: Day 010)
11. Closures, `functools.wraps` & parametrised decorators in depth (done: Day 012)
12. `pathlib`, file I/O & `logging` (done: Day 011)
13. `asyncio` basics (done: Day 013)
14. Concurrency: threads vs processes (done: Day 014)
15. Performance & big-O, profiling

Order may adapt to progress.
